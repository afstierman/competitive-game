Field Guide

Controls

  • WASD / Arrow keys — Move
  • Mouse — Aim your sword
  • Left click — Slash with the sword; hold at least 0.25 seconds, then release to fire the slingshot farther
  • Q — Switch between purchased sword and slingshot
  • E — Open the shop when nearby
  • Esc — Close a panel

Current goal

Explore the sectors, collect crystals for cycles, and return to the central hub to buy stronger swords, armor, and healing. Learn each creature’s weakness in the almanac and survive as far as you can.
