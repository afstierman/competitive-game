Computer Bug

A small pursuer with little armor. It closes in and attacks at close range.

Sunken Abyss

Pool Octopus

Deep water

Its tentacle reaches toward you, then reels you in and holds you still. The tentacle cannot be cut; defeat the octopus to break free.

HP 3DMG 2

Red Crab

Water and land

Its shell protects the front. Circle around and strike from behind to get through its armor.

HP 4DMG 2

Violet Circuit

Computer Virus

Fast and hard to pin down. It blinks to the opposite side of you, and a magenta marker shows where it will land. It cannot be hit while it is teleporting.

HP 10DMG 4

Ember Wastes

Skeleton

A fast chaser that swings its sword in a two-hit combo and lunges forward with each swing. A red zone shows where it will strike. Sidestep once the aim locks, then hit it while it recovers. It avoids lava.

HP 20DMG 8

Magma Slug

Lava pools

Keeps its distance and spits lava globs that leave burning puddles. Rings on the ground show where each glob lands. Standing in a puddle hurts badly, so get out fast. It is quick on lava but slow on land, and its death leaves lava puddles behind.

HP 20DMG 10PUDDLE 5
